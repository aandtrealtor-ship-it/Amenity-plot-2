Why Igatpuri

Land that sits between two cities tends to hold its value better than land that sits beside one.

Igatpuri takes the monsoon full on. It is the green belt Mumbai drives to, and it is close enough to Nashik to be a weekday place rather than only a weekend one. The Samruddhi Expressway changed the arithmetic: what used to be a long drive is now a short one.

Two kinds of buyer end up here. Investors who want land in a corridor that is still being built out, and families who want a place to put up a second home in a few years' time. The plots suit both, which is why the sizes run from 2,000 to 10,000 sq ft.

Come and walk it before you decide. Bring someone who knows land.
